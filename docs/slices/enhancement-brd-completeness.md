# Enhancement — A complete Business Requirements Document

**Status: Approved 2026-10-30; Phase A in progress on `feat/business-requirements-document-export`.**
Follows `docs/slices/enhancement-business-requirements-document-export.md` (ADR-0119), which ships
the document. This slice closes the gaps between that document and a standard BRD (IIBA BABOK
requirement types; the common Asana, Lucid, Slite, Bakkah, monday.com, Smartsheet and Info-Tech
templates) in three phases, each a PR that leaves the product usable, cheapest first.

Owner decisions (2026-10-30): execute A, B and C in order; priority per business requirement
(Phase D) stays unscheduled; non-functional requirements and business use cases are kept only when
cited to a source passage; journey steps stay out of the document (they are reviewer advice,
ADR-0097, never confirmed data).

**Scope.** Phases B and C are ordinary engineering work under `AGENTS.md`: domain, prompts, API,
persistence codecs and screens change. Phase A is document-only. Frontend feature work is declared
in each phase.

---

## Objective

Make the exported Business Requirements Document complete by the standard's measure, without ever
printing anything a person has not confirmed or approved: add the sections the data already
supports, capture the intake facts a BRD expects, and teach the analysis the two requirement
categories it does not know yet, non-functional requirements and business use cases.

## User Outcome

- A stakeholder opening the Word or Markdown document finds a version history, the actors and
  their scenarios, a Risks section, what is out of scope, the current and proposed way of working,
  the non-functional requirements with their type, and the business use cases, each numbered.
- The Requirement Owner reviews non-functional requirements and business use cases on Clarify and
  Confirm like facts and rules, so what the document prints is confirmed, not inferred.

## The gaps this closes

| Gap (standard BRD) | Phase | How |
|---|---|---|
| Version history table | A | Requirement and breakdown revision history, labelled by what changed |
| Actors and scenarios overview | A | Story roles and goals grouped by actor, once the backlog is approved |
| Risks as a top-level section | A | Review risks, once the backlog is approved |
| Out of scope | B | New intake list field |
| Current state / proposed state | B | New intake text fields |
| Non-functional requirements (NF-nn, typed) | C | New analysis category, owner-confirmed, cited only |
| Business use cases (BUC-nn) | C | New analysis category, owner-confirmed, cited only |

Deliberate omissions, also recorded in ADR-0119: business journeys affected (advisory data),
transition requirements, cost-benefit and budget, timeline, a traceability matrix from functional to
business requirements (no feature-to-fact links exist), a written executive summary (nothing in the
document is generated by a model), and priority per business requirement.

## In Scope

- Phase A: document schema 1.1; version history, actors and scenarios, Risks; numbering 1–18.
- Phase B: `current_state`, `proposed_state`, `out_of_scope` through domain, use cases, codec, API,
  analysis prompt (v27), screens, History diff, knowledge chunks and the document (schema 1.2).
- Phase C: two analysis categories through domain, analyzer port and schema, prompt v28, mappers,
  fake analyzer, packet consolidation, codec, API, review fingerprint, generation prompts (one bump:
  `epic-v5`, `feature-v9`, `story-v9`, also carrying B's fields), knowledge index, Confirm screen and
  the document (schema 1.3); ADR-0120 and an ADR-0029 amendment.

## Out of Scope

- Any model-written prose in the document.
- Clarification questions about non-functional requirements or business use cases; they are settled
  categories like facts, not uncertainties.
- Journey steps in the document; linking business use cases to catalogue journeys.
- New contract fields for actors or risks: both are rendered from the approved backlog.
- Diffing requirement fields other than the three new ones in History.
- Phase D, priority per business requirement.

## Business Rules

- Nothing in the document is generated at export time; every section is copied from confirmed or
  approved data, or states that the data is not recorded.
- Actors, scenarios and risks appear only once the backlog has formal final approval, like
  functional requirements.
- Non-functional requirements and business use cases are settled categories, kept only when cited,
  carried whole into each round, confirmed with the analysis; never clarifications.
- A business use case is written at business level: an external actor, an optional trigger, a goal
  and the steps the business performs; no systems or screens.
- Empty new fields are omitted from payloads, fingerprints and context tokens, so existing
  approvals, goldens and live tokens stay valid.
- The intake fields are optional and never change analysis eligibility.
